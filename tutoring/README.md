# Tutoring — optional track

An alignment track: the mechanics and thermal-analysis background the course builds on but
has no room to teach.

**Optional.** The course does not assume you attended, and nothing you need for the
mandatory project block lives only here.

The modules are published one at a time, each shortly before the lectures it prepares.
This repository currently holds the first.

| | module | h | notebook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1** | Continuum mechanics | 2.5 | `M1_continuum` — traction and the Cauchy tensor, equilibrium, principal stresses and invariants, Mohr's circle, hydrostatic and deviatoric parts, strain and compatibility, Saint-Venant |

The notebook needs no install. It is plain Python (numpy, matplotlib, scipy): no
bootstrap cell, nothing to set up. It runs on a bare Colab runtime or on any local
Python.

## The required background: Ye

The background to be studied independently is *Structural and Stress Analysis: Theories,
Tutorials and Examples* (J. Ye). Several books cover this material and they do not always
use the same symbols or sign conventions; **where they overlap, follow Ye**, because that
is the one you are asked to read.

For M1 that is chapter **1** (forces, stress, strain, Hooke), chapter **7** (two-dimensional
stress and Mohr's circle) and **8.1** (strain transformation). Section 7 of the notebook is
his **2.7**. Chapters **4** and **8.2**, beam diagrams and strain gauges, are reading only.

## The hours are indicative, and the notebook is deliberately over-full

M1 covers its material completely, with more worked content than 2.5 hours can cover out
loud. That is the opposite of trimming it to fit the slot.

The track opens with a **diagnostic test**, and what it shows decides *what gets skipped in
the room* — not what has been deleted from the file. A cohort that is solid on index
notation gets that section as reading; a cohort that is not gets it worked through. Either
way the material is there afterwards, which matters precisely because the track is
optional: a student who could not attend still has the whole thing.
